FAQ

Doubts resolved before the first call.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Month to month has a 3-month minimum, then 30 days' notice. With the frozen price (6 months), leaving early costs 1.5 monthly payments or the remaining months, whichever is less — never more.

Who owns the code?

You do. Code and deliverables are transferred to the client. We don't create dependency — if another team takes over tomorrow, they can.

What if a bug is introduced — who pays?

If it's 100% on us (unauthorized changes, something we broke), we fix it at no cost. If the change was approved by you and the bug is reported after 15 days, it's handled as normal hours from your plan.

What's not included?

Infrastructure, licenses and third-party services, and projects outside your plan's scope — those are quoted separately.

Can you work with my existing tech team?

Absolutely. We join your repos, processes and tools. We work as an extension of your team, not as a separate island.

How do I know what was done each month?

You get a monthly report with hours used, tasks executed and progress. You can also check it anytime in your cometax.click account.

Freeze your price for 6 months

List prices are updated every semester for exchange rate, tooling costs and inflation. With the 6-month contract your monthly fee stays fixed for the whole term, and if you renew you keep that same price for another 6 months.
